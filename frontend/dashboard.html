How Blog Writer works

One keyword becomes a publish-ready article in four stages.

  1. SERP — competitor titles, headings, and related queries.
  2. Brief — title, search intent, topics, and structure from your settings.
  3. Draft — one generation pass, grounded on your brand facts when you add a site.
  4. SEO — meta, FAQs, and SEO / GEO / AEO scores.
