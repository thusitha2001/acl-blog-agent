How to Create a High-Quality Blog Article

Use the fields below to give the Blog Agent enough context to create a useful, SEO-friendly, publish-ready article.

Main Keyword

Enter the main search keyword you want the article to target.

Example: summer outfit ideas

Tip: Use one primary keyword. The agent can identify related keywords and topics from search results.

Search Intent

Choose why someone would search this keyword. The article is written to match that intent.

Informational: they want to learn. Commercial: they are comparing options. Transactional: they are ready to buy or act. Navigational: they are looking for a specific brand, page, or product.

Title

Enter your preferred article title.

If you leave this empty, the agent can generate a suitable title based on search intent and competitor analysis.

Target Audience

Describe who the article is written for.

Example: US teenage girls and young women looking for affordable, comfortable summer outfit ideas

Tip: Be specific about location, age group, interests, or buying intent when relevant.

Target Words

Choose the approximate article length.

Example: 1200 words

Tip: Don't choose an unnecessarily large word count. Let the search intent determine how much information the article needs.

Tone

Choose the writing style that matches your audience and brand.

Examples: Friendly, Casual, Professional, Educational

Article Type

Choose the format that best matches the keyword.

Examples: How-to Guide, Listicle, Buying Guide, Informational Article

If you're unsure, use Auto-detect.

Point of View

Choose how the article should address the reader.

For most consumer blogs, Second Person is a good choice because it naturally uses "you" and "your."

Readability

Choose the reading level that matches your audience.

For most general consumer content, 6th–8th grade readability provides a good balance between clarity and useful information.

Structure

Enable the sections that are useful for your article.

  • FAQ: Useful for answering common search questions.
  • Key Takeaways: Useful for summarizing the most important points.
  • Conclusion: Useful for closing the article and reinforcing the main message.

Hook

Choose how the article should begin.

A question works well for many informational topics.

Example: What should you wear when the summer heat starts to rise?

Brand

Enter your brand name and website.

Example: All Cotton and Linen

Adding your website helps the article stay aligned with your brand and allows relevant internal links to be used.

Internal Links

Add relevant pages from your website.

Recommended format:

Anchor text | URL

Example: women's high hip T-shirt | https://example.com/product-page

Tip: Only add pages that are genuinely relevant to the article. The agent will place links naturally where they provide value to the reader.

Brand Voice

Describe how you want your brand to sound.

Example: Friendly, practical, modern, natural, and helpful without sounding overly promotional.

Additional Instructions

Use this field for article-specific requirements that are not covered by the other settings.

Good examples:

  • Write naturally for a US audience.
  • Use practical examples.
  • Keep the content conversational and specific.
  • Make the article SEO, AEO, and GEO friendly.
  • Avoid keyword stuffing and repetitive wording.

Best Practice

For the best results, provide a clear keyword, specific target audience, relevant internal links, and a short description of your preferred brand voice.

The Blog Agent will use these inputs together with search and competitor analysis to create the article.
