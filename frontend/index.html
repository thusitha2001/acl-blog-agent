How Blog Agent Works

  1. 1

    Enter a keyword

    Start with the topic or phrase you want to rank for (e.g. "how to choose linen table runners"). The system builds the entire article around this.

  2. 2

    SERP analysis

    Blog Agent analyzes your keyword's real search results: what competitors cover, which headings they use, related queries people ask, and ranking content gaps.

  3. 3

    Auto content brief

    The AI drafts a structured brief: title, target word count, required topics, secondary keywords, reader questions, and your formatting choices.

  4. 4

    Research grounding (optional)

    If you enter a Website URL, the system scrapes that site at request time so the article stays grounded on your brand's real content. Used for that one article only — never stored.

  5. 5

    Single-call article generation

    One model call writes the full article in Markdown, plus SEO metadata: meta title, meta description, FAQs, and image alt text. Output streams progressively so you watch it happen.

  6. 6

    Validation & quality checks

    Every draft is checked for word count, exactly one H1, clean Markdown, fact accuracy (no invented statistics/pricing), keyword repetition, and original wording.

Pro tips

  • Target Words lets you request any length from 300 to 8,000 words — it overrides the preset Article Size.
  • Additional Instructions, Hook Brief, and Brand Voice are capped by word count to keep the brief focused.
  • Website field triggers a fresh scrape for that request only — great for grounding on your latest content.
