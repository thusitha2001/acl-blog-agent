How Blog Agent Works

1

Enter a keyword

Start with the topic or phrase you want to rank for (e.g. "how to choose linen table runners"). The system builds the entire article around this.

2

SERP analysis

Blog Agent analyzes your keyword's real search results: what competitors cover, which headings they use, related queries people ask, and ranking content gaps.

3

Auto content brief

The AI drafts a structured brief: title, target word count, required topics, secondary keywords, reader questions, and your formatting choices (H3s, tables, lists, hooks).

4

Research grounding (optional)

If you enter a Website URL, the system scrapes that site at request time so the article stays grounded on your brand's real content. It is used for that one article only and never stored.

5

Single-call article generation

One model call writes the full article in Markdown, plus SEO metadata: meta title, meta description, FAQs, and image alt text. Output streams to you progressively so you watch it happen.

6

Validation & quality checks

Every draft is checked for word count, exactly one H1, clean Markdown (lists and tables), fact accuracy (no invented statistics or pricing), keyword repetition, and original wording before it reaches you.

Pro tips

  • Target Words lets you request any length from 300 to 8,000 words — it overrides the preset Article Size.
  • Additional Instructions, Hook Brief, and Brand Voice are capped by word count to keep the brief focused.
  • Your User ID is attached automatically when you're logged in, so you can track which articles belong to which user.
