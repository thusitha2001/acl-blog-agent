How to Rewrite a Blog Article

Use Blog Rewriter when you already have an article and want a fresh, original version that keeps the same facts.

Existing Article

Paste the full post — HTML or plain text. This is the only required field.

Tip: Longer, complete drafts rewrite more cleanly than short snippets.

Main Keyword

Optional. If empty, the rewriter infers a keyword from the title or source.

Title, Tone, Audience

Use these when you want a different headline, voice, or reader than the original.

Target Words

Leave empty to stay close to the original length. Set a number only if you want the rewrite shorter or longer.

Additional Instructions

Call out anything that must stay: product names, internal links, claims you do not want expanded.
